### ABOUT NYX

**NYX™** is an independent development team creating a **persistent, player-driven Star Wars roleplay universe** inspired by *Star Wars: The Old Republic*.

Our ambition is to build a living galaxy where **decisions, alliances, and conflicts shape its future**.

### FORGE YOUR PATH

Choose your allegiance and write your own story.

- **Sith Empire** → Power and domination.
- **Galactic Republic** → Freedom and diplomacy.
- **Hutt Cartel** → Wealth and influence.
- **Independent Factions** → Freedom to forge your own destiny.

**Political intrigue, diplomacy, espionage, and warfare** form the foundation of an evolving galaxy shaped by its players.

### OUR VISION

Preserve the rich legacy of **Star Wars: The Old Republic** while giving players the freedom to create new stories within its universe.

NYX will initially launch as a **French-speaking roleplay community**, with plans for future international expansion.
